AS5003 · Stockholm University · Autumn 2026

Cosmology

Lecturer: Sambit Kumar Giri

Schedule, homework and announcements are on Canvas.

Book: B. Ryden, Introduction to Cosmology, 2nd ed.

Lectures

  • Lecture 1 Introduction and the fundamental observations Course information, a short history of cosmology, and the introduction to fundamental observations: the dark night sky, homogeneity and isotropy, the CMB, light elements and Hubble's law. Slides
  • Lecture 2 Measuring distances and velocities; cosmic expansion Redshift, the Hubble–Lemaître law, H₀ from supernovae and the Hubble time. Slides
  • Lecture 3 Curvature of space and the Robertson–Walker metric Metrics, special and general relativity, curved space, the Robertson–Walker metric and proper distance. Slides
  • Lecture 4 Distances and the Friedmann equations The horizon distance, Einstein's field equation, the Friedmann, fluid and acceleration equations, and equations of state. Slides

Reference

  • Notation and conventions The symbols and distance names we use (Ryden's), and the benchmark model.
